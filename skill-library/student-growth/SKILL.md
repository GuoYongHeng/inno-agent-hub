# Student Growth

Convert observations and contacts into traceable `growth_event` or `parent_contact` records.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the student identity and event date.
2. Separate observed facts, teacher interpretation, and proposed next action.
3. Preserve evidence and recorder provenance.
4. Query longitudinal history before drafting a summary.
5. Keep sensitive details restricted and show only what the task requires.

## Rules

- Do not infer a diagnosis or label from a single observation.
- Use neutral, behavior-based language in generated records.
- Ask for confirmation before sending or publishing any communication.
